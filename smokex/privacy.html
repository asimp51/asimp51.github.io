Privacy policy: SmokeX: Cigarette Simulator

Effective 24 November 2026. This policy covers the SmokeX: Cigarette Simulator app for Android and iOS (the "app", package com.smokesim.virtualsmoke), published by the SmokeX developer ("we").

Summary

  • The app is for adults only (18+). It has no account, no sign-in and no chat.
  • Your age answer, settings and progress stay on your device.
  • The microphone is used only to measure how hard you blow. Audio is never recorded, stored or sent.
  • We use Google Firebase for anonymous usage statistics and crash reports, and Google AdMob to show ads.
  • We do not sell your personal data.

1. Stored only on your device

The month and year you enter on the age screen, your settings, unlocked items, achievements, daily challenge progress, smoke-free counter and purchase status are stored locally. We do not receive them. Under-18 answers lock the app on that device.

2. Microphone

If you tap the microphone button and allow access, the app measures the loudness of your breath in real time so the virtual smoke reacts. The sound is analysed in memory and discarded. It is not recorded, saved or transmitted. You can turn the microphone off at any time in the app or in your device settings.

3. Analytics and crash reports (Google Firebase)

We use Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics and Remote Config. They collect information such as app interactions (for example which item you opened or that a purchase started), device model, operating system version, language, approximate location derived from the IP address, crash logs, and an app-instance identifier. We use this to fix crashes and improve the app. Firebase's own privacy information: firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version shows banner, interstitial and rewarded ads from Google AdMob. AdMob and its partners may collect your advertising ID, IP address, device information and ad interactions to serve and measure ads, and, where you allow it, to personalise them. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland the app asks for your consent first, and you can change your choice in Settings (Privacy options). You can also reset or limit your advertising ID in your device settings. See policies.google.com/technologies/ads. Buying "Remove Ads" or "Premium" turns off banner and interstitial ads. Rewarded ads, which you choose to watch, stay available.

5. Purchases

One-time purchases are handled by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We do not see or store your payment details. The store tells the app which purchases you own so it can unlock them.

6. Sharing a picture

If you use the camera or share button, the app creates an image on your device and opens your system share sheet. We do not receive the image. Where it goes is up to you and the app you share it with.

7. Children

The app is not for anyone under 18 and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. Tobacco-related content in the app is simulated and the app does not encourage real smoking. Smoking seriously harms your health. Nothing in the app is medical advice.

8. Retention and your rights

Analytics and crash data are kept by Google for the periods set in Firebase. Local data is removed when you uninstall the app. Depending on where you live (for example the EEA, UK or California) you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data, to object to processing and to withdraw consent. Because we cannot identify you from the data we hold, requests about advertising data are best made through your device's advertising settings or Google's own controls. To ask us anything, use the contact below.

9. International transfers

Google may process data in countries other than your own, under its standard safeguards.

10. Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the new version.
